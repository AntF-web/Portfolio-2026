BIO

“SPACE IS THE BREATH OF ART”— Frank Lloyd Wright

Raised in an urban musical and graphic environment, I have spent years moving between cultures, creative practices and underground scenes.

My different lives have led me through punk bass, photography, dub experiments, activist DJing, video editing, artist management, education and social work. I like precise tools, strong ideas, simple interfaces and projects that keep some human disorder inside the system.
